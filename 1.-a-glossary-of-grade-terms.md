# A Glossary of GRADE terms



Instructions for Using the Glossary

* The glossary is divided into thematic categories.
* The content page below shows the categories and terms which are hyperlinked.
* Additionally, each category features a _list of terms at the top of the page_, all of which are hyperlinked.

_To navigate to a specific term's definition, hold down the Ctrl key (or Command key on Mac) and click on the term._

Some terms are relevant to multiple categories and appear more than once.

* In such cases, the term is defined only once in its primary section.
* Subsequent mentions include _a reference number_ that directs you to the section where the term is first defined.
* To navigate to the _primary definition_, hold down _Ctrl (or Command)_ and click on the reference number.

Within the definitions:

* &#x20;Words that are _italicized_ and _bold_ are meant for emphasis.
* Terms that are _bold and blue_ are hyperlinked and will take you to the original section where the term is defined.

_Use these hyperlinks and references for efficient navigation and comprehension of the glossary._
