# 15.Evidence to Decision: Example of a Structured Evidence-to-Decision framework

This appendix provides a worked example of a structured Evidence-to-Decision (EtD) framework which explicitly considers each primary and secondary decision factors separately in moving from evidence to recommendations. The framework is presented in two formats produced using online platforms for digitally-structured guideline content: one using GRADEPro, and the second using MAGICapp.

_Example:_ Consider a guideline panel is interested in producing practice guidance regarding the management of hospitalized adults with community-acquired pneumonia. The panel prioritizes the effects of short-term systemic corticosteroids, relative to no systemic corticosteroids, as a question of interest. Anticipating treatment effects are very likely to differ between patients with more severe pneumonia and less severe pneumonia, they construct separate PICOs for each patient group.&#x20;

The research team supporting the panel searches for relevant literature, and identifies a recent systematic review of randomized controlled trials that compared short-term systemic corticosteroids versus standard care of placebo (without systemic corticosteroids) in adults hospitalized with suspected or probable community-acquired pneumonia.<sup>1</sup> The meta-analysis included all severities of disease and included a pre-specified sub-group analysis of more severe versus less severe pneumonia, where trials were defined as having patients with more severe disease if 50% or more of patients had severe pneumonia scores or were admitted to the intensive care unit at time of randomization (and were otherwise classified as having patients with less severe disease). The review identified 18 eligible studies including 4,661 patients. Results showed that corticosteroids probably reduce mortality in more severe pneumonia (relative risk \[RR] 0.62, 95% confidence intervals \[CI] 0.45 to 0.85, moderate certainty), and possibly have little or no effect in less severe pneumonia (RR 1.08, 95% CI 0.83 to 1.42, low certainty).
