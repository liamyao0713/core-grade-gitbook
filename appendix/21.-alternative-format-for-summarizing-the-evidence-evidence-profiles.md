# 21. Alternative format for summarizing the evidence- Evidence Profiles

Evidence profiles, an alternative format for presenting summaries of evidence, contain the same key information as Summary of Findings Tables (SoFs). Evidence profiles have additional columns with decisions for each of the domains of certainty of evidence and may have a column specifying the importance of each outcome.
